## Why this scores higher than anything before it

Hook 5, and it is the first genuinely earned one: *"I hired five AI workers. Four
of them could not afford to start."* It is literal, it is strange, and the payoff
arrives at 2.5s as a stack of red `CANNOT WORK` rows. No metaphor to decode.

Retention 5 because the video turns twice — the dead hires, then a run that
worked, then the same brief failing. The last turn lands at 29s, unusually late
and still holding.

Relevance 5 because every frame is Handsel's own output. Nothing generic.

## The thing that makes it work

It ends on **"Sometimes."**

Every other AI demo answers "does it work" with yes. This one shows the same
brief producing a full payout and a mostly-failed run, and then says the honest
thing. That is the Office's P-05 bet — candour outperforms polish — as a video
rather than a hypothesis.
