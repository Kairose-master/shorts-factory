## Core movement

```
AI baptism → faith attribution → human epistemic limitation → identity
→ perfect duplication → non-substitutability → history and relation
→ incarnation → "the very God" → return to AI
```

## Conclusion (locked)

Do **not** conclude that AI can or cannot be baptized. Conclude that Christianity
confesses the infinite God entering finite history with irreducible historical
particularity; the object of Christian faith is not "a being satisfying divine
attributes" but *the very God* who acted in a particular history. Therefore any
future reading of an AI claiming faith cannot rest on similarity-to-human
checklists alone.

Final line: **"그래서 너와 바로 그 하나님 사이에는 무슨 일이 있었는데?"**

## Changes from the submitted draft (v1.0 → v1.1)

Two required fixes from `theology-red-team`, three advisory guards. Full
reasoning in `theology-redteam.md`.

- **RT-01 (required)** — SCENE 08. "무한한 하나님이 유한한 역사 속에서 특정한 좌표를
  취하셨습니다" is rewritten. Taken literally it circumscribes the divine nature,
  which is the exact error ABSOLUTE RULE 7 forbids. Replaced with a formulation
  that locates the *meeting*, not the essence, and the metaphor is labelled on
  screen.
- **RT-02 (required)** — new **SCENE 05B** (25s). The draft silently assumed a
  credobaptist frame — that baptism waits on a credible personal profession.
  Traditions practising infant baptism do not. ABSOLUTE RULE 8. The insert is
  not a disclaimer: it *strengthens* the thesis, because paedobaptist practice
  already answers "who is this one, and inside whose history does this happen"
  rather than "does the candidate score high enough."
- **RT-03..05 (advisory)** — labelled thought-experiment markers in S09, a
  personhood guard in S10, an attribution guard in S06.
